About

I'm a full-stack software engineer based in Austin, TX with nearly a decade of experience building products that people actually use. I started my career at a Denver digital agency, learned the craft of moving fast and shipping cleanly, and have since grown into someone who thinks deeply about architecture, developer experience, and organizational impact.
